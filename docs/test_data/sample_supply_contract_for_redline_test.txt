MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT (TEST DRAFT)

This Master Supply Agreement is entered into between Aero Components India Pvt. Ltd. ("Supplier")
and Flight Systems Global Ltd. ("Buyer").

1) Limitation of Liability
Supplier liability shall not exceed 200% of contract value. Consequential damages may be claimed by either party.

2) Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York, and parties submit to courts in New York.

3) Dispute Resolution
Any dispute will be resolved by courts, and either party may seek litigation as first remedy.

4) Price
Prices are subject to escalation at Supplier discretion based on market conditions.

5) Force Majeure
Force majeure includes shortage of labor, economic hardship, and cash-flow disruptions. Payment obligations may be suspended.

6) Liquidated Damages
Liquidated damages are 1.5% per week of total order value with no overall cap.

7) Orders Extending Beyond Termination
All existing orders continue for 24 months after termination at pre-termination prices.

8) Quantity Protection
Forecasts are non-binding, and Buyer accepts all inventory built against any forecast variance.

9) Inventory Requirements
Buyer shall maintain minimum 16 weeks of inventory at all times.

10) Change Orders
Supplier may implement engineering changes immediately and pricing impacts will be discussed later.

END OF TEST DRAFT
